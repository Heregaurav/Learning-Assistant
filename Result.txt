Player Controls: Play/pause, +/-10-second skip, speed selection (0.25×, 0.5×, 0.75×, 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 1.75×, 2×), volume/mute, and fullscreen.
Reliable Video Progress Tracking:
Formula: $\text{Actual watch percentage} = \min\left(100, \frac{\text{Verified watched seconds}}{\text{Total video duration}} \times 100\right)$.
Prevents skipping to the end from giving completion credit by tracking verified playback ranges and elapsed play time.
Preserves and restores the student's playback position (lastPositionSeconds) across sessions.
Completion status is derived by comparing actual watch progress against the teacher-configured threshold (watchPercent >= completionThreshold).
Teacher Analytics Integration:
Integrated into the existing Analytics tab (ClassroomInsights.tsx) under a dedicated Lecture Progress section.
Lecture-Level Metrics: Enrolled students count, completed count, in-progress count, not-started count, average watch percentage, and tracking capability indicator.
Individual Student Progress: Expandable drilldown listing every enrolled student with their watch percentage bar and threshold-evaluated completion status.
